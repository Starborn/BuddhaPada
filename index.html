About the book

Footprints of the Buddha, the Buddhapada, have been carved and honoured across Buddhist Asia for more than two thousand years. This book gathers photographs of footprints from Gandhara, India, Myanmar and Singapore, a reading on the meaning of their symbols in Buddhist and Vedic literature, short verses and koans, and full-page drawings to colour.

  • Catalogue of images
  • Meaning of the symbols
  • Colouring book
  • Verses and koans
  • Glossary and the 108 marks
  • Other footprints: Milarepa, Guru Rinpoche, Lakshmi

Printing instructions

This book contains images and symbols considered sacred. It is published as a PDF and made freely available as a gift of Dharma. It can be printed in full or in part, and the drawings coloured on paper, best on A4 or A3. If printed, please do not place the copy on the floor or under other objects, and dispose of it with consideration, if necessary by offering it to others or to fire.

The gift of the Dharma surpasses all gifts.

Dhammapada 354
